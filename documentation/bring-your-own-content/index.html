Bring your own content

Your application owns the content. Axiomatic arranges the relationships.

These are ordinary links and buttons, with their own behavior. Follow the investigation, open a destination, then change the available space. The controls stay themselves as their arrangement changes.

First, one group of actions

Every action is a member of one Switcher. They share a row when all fit; otherwise they form one column.

Then, keep related actions together

The secondary actions form a group of their own. As space narrows, the outer group becomes a column first. The inner group can keep its row until it too needs more room.

What this experiment establishes

The supported members are non-wrapping actions at their natural width. They may overflow a slot narrower than an individual action. Wrapping prose, images, and independently bounded components need their own sizing contract. The registration API is experimental.

This example uses application-owned DOM and the layout adapter. It does not load the playground, its editor, or the controlled text renderer.
